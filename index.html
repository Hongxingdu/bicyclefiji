Bike Rentals

Reliable bike hire options tailored for scenic day trips, local cruising, and eco-tours around the Nadi area. Perfect for tourists and independent adventurers wanting to take in the scenery at their own pace while minimizing their environmental footprint.

Guided Tours

Experience Fiji's stunning coastal views and vibrant local culture with expert-led cycling tours. Designed for all fitness levels, these guided routes take riders off the beaten track through local villages, scenic coastal roads around Nadi, and lush tropical landscapes, offering an authentic, immersive way to explore the island safely.

Reliable bike repair and maintenance services to keep your cycling adventures smooth and safe.
